Texture pack

Dolphin-style packs (files named tex1_..._.dds). The TV's graphics chip can show ASTC 4x4 or uncompressed DDS files, but not BC1/BC3/BC7 (DXT), the format most packs ship in; those files are skipped. Convert such packs to ASTC first (for example with the ACGC Android port's converter).
